### Affected App, Adopter, Users, or Systems

Precedent's citation check (the safeguard agent, `app/agents.py`) and its engine-side claim checker, on every answer shown to readers. The reader sees "Citations checked" or "Citation check failed" on each memo.

### Observed Consequence and Workaround

Nothing measures how accurately the claim-support check separates supported from unsupported claims on Colorado legal answers. The check runs the engine's MiniCheck-RoBERTa-Large scorer (about 1.2 GB resident). Two alternatives are already on this owner's machine and unevaluated: fastino/GLiNER2.5-Decide and GLiNER2.5-multi-Decide (340M / 287M parameters, Apache-2.0). Fastino's own judge benchmark lists balanced accuracy 0.58 and AUROC 0.52 for Decide, close to chance, so it must not be assumed to work. A live answer on 2026-11-01 (run 073043-d0159c) failed the check only because of a sentence-splitting bug (citations written after the full stop; fixed in PR #160), which shows false alarms were also unmeasured. Workaround: none; the check's verdict is shown to readers as it is.

### Risk if the Observation Remains Unresolved
