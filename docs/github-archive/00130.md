# Issue #130: Restore the v1 working-page capabilities (O73-O79)

- State: open
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-19
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/arize-ai-hackersprint/issues/130

The owner asked that the capabilities of the earlier product exist in the rebuilt one, produced by the builder rather than patched in by hand, and focused on use. They need not look the same.

Seven capabilities are missing today. Each is stated as an outcome in `docs/specs/2026-10-18-frontend-v3.md` and gated by `tests/test_frontend_v3_features.py`.

- **O73** While a run works, the page names the pipeline stage and shows the answer as it is written; the finished memo replaces the draft. Region named `Run progress`.
- **O74** The source list states each authority's type, court, date and citation, and shows a retrieved passage; an authority with no passage says it came from the citation graph.
- **O75** The memo head states whether the citation check passed and how many references were verified, how long the run took, and whether the answer came from a cache or an offline template. Region named `Answer status`.
- **O76** A first visit offers a dismissible getting-started cue with example questions; the dismissal is remembered. An empty ask announces `Question needed`.
- **O77** Clicking a pin opens the details of the authority it stands for, one at a time, closed by Escape. Region named `Authority`. The map contract gains `pinCites(i)`.
- **O78** `Mute` silences all narration and becomes `Unmute`; Escape stops the voice and the tour; a clip being fetched shows `Audio loading`; a queued clip never cuts off the one playing.
- **O79** From Home the reader can open a saved run without typing an address; the memo, map and tour load for it. Action named `Open a saved run`, panel named `Saved runs`.

Baseline on the current build: all seven gates fail, each for the reason above.
