Items 2, 3 and 4, measured. Data committed beside each section.

**3. GLiNER as a ranking signal** — 387 query-document pairs over 8 development queries. Mean Spearman against the engine's order **−0.101**, mean top-10 overlap **1.9 of 10**. It produces an unrelated ordering, not a sharper one. At 1301 ms/pair, rescoring 50 documents costs ~65 s per query against a 1.1 s warm engine: sixty times the latency for two documents out of ten in common. Recommendation: don't.

**2. eyecite** — 165 citations across 200 pooled passages, roughly twice what a naive reporter regex finds, because it resolves short forms, `id.` and `supra`. The CAP comparison is **not** done: `~/precedent/data/edges.jsonl` is a case-to-case citation graph, not inline citation strings, so it needs a resolver rather than a counter. Stated as a gap rather than a result.

**4. 80-question regression** — two back-to-back captures at production settings: **identical top-12 on 80 of 80**, identical full lists on 80 of 80, same corpus and graph revision. Warm latency 3865 ms vs 1089 ms p50 between the runs, entirely because the first ran engine-cold. The OS page cache was not dropped — that needs root — and the doc says so.
