### Affected App, Adopter, Users, or Systems

Precedent's citation guard (`check_named` in `app/agents.py`) and the engine's citation parser, on every answer.

### Observed Consequence and Workaround

The guard spots authority-shaped strings in a memo with a short list of regular expressions (U.S.C., C.F.R., P.2d, P., U.S., F.2d/3d/4th, B.R., C.R.S.). Colorado-specific forms (for example Colo. App. and the new public-domain COA/CO cites) and case names are not covered by that list, so an invented authority in an uncovered form may pass, and a real one may be flagged. The engine already prefers eyecite when installed (`legal_graph/engine/pipeline.py`, `eyecite_citations`) but the app's guard does not use it. No precision or recall has been measured for the regexes, eyecite, or a zero-shot GLiNER model. Workaround: none.
