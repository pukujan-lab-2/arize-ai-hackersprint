# Issue #127: v3: composer at the bottom, and the map pans and zooms (O53-O57)

- State: closed (2026-10-19)
- Opened by Pukujan on 2026-10-19
- Original: https://github.com/Pukujan-lab/arize-ai-hackersprint/issues/127

## Objective

The owner named four faults in the v3 cutover: the composer sits at the top of the memo pane, `/map` has no composer on a phone, the map has no zoom controls, and the map cannot be dragged or swiped.

The spec is the authority. The implementation stays inside `web/`.

## Inputs

- `docs/specs/2026-10-18-frontend-v3.md` — outcomes O53-O57 and the accessible-name table
- `tests/test_frontend_v3_map_chat.py` — the behaviour the page must show

## Outcomes

- O53 — the composer sits below the memo and below the map at every width; memo controls stay at the top.
- O54 — the composer is visible with the map pane shown, on a phone and at `/map`; its question renders a memo.
- O55 — `Zoom in`, `Zoom out` and `Reset view` change the camera.
- O56 — a mouse drag or a finger swipe pans the camera and never focuses a pin or starts a tour.
- O57 — the wheel zooms around the pointer.

## Evidence

Built in `a39de2d` on main. `tests/test_frontend_v3_map_chat.py` plus the home, map, layout and tour suites pass (118 tests).

## Out of scope

Observability and slides.
