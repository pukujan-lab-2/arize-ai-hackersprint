### Observed Consequence and Workaround

Observed: Mid-tour, the prev, speaker, Tour and next controls are tiny in the bottom right with no labels or progress. A county label ('Baca') renders on top of the speaker button, the floating 'TAP TO TALK' mic covers the court list, and a county tooltip ('Yuma County FIPS 08125, no retrieved cases') lingers. The caption box repeats the sidebar summary word for word. The stats say '0 counties linked' while pins and citations [1]-[8] are shown, because unknown courts are pinned to Denver (#34).
Expected: Larger labeled tour controls with a 'Stop N of M' indicator; nothing drawn over the controls or the court list; tooltips hide when the pointer leaves; the caption is a short current-stop line (or removed); honest stats wording; a short note when pin locations are approximate; a first-step hint for new visitors (ask or pick a run, then press Tour) and a clear empty state; the map shows the current run's name with a 'Change run' link to /runs.
