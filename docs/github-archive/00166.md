# PR #166: Stop checking quotations against themselves, and record what the measurements show

Two parts: a findings doc for the measurements, and the safeguard change those measurements justify.

## Measurements

Twenty development queries, four captures, both arms inside one corpus revision: graph on and graph off return **identical result lists on all 20**, at `k=50`, reranking off and on. The graph stage reports 25 additions across 5 queries; none reach the results, because neighbours score 0.0 and sort below every hybrid candidate before the cut.

Replaying 20 stored answers through the old and new sentence splitter: only 2 were flagged by the old one, none became clean, and **all 20 still fail**. The formatting fix (#160) is real but is not the cause.

## The change

Per-claim scoring showed the cause: across ten runs **36 of 50 claims were verbatim quotations of their own cited passage**, scored as unsupported because the checker was asked whether a quote entails itself. Claims whose quotation appears word for word in the cited text are now marked `verbatim` and skipped. `_cited_span` also returns a window around the quote instead of the quote alone.

## What it does not do

The pass rate does not move: 0 of 10 before, 0 of 10 after. The remaining one to three prose claims per run score 0.09-0.41, and the aggregation is a mean, so one weak claim pulls the answer under threshold.

**The gate is right about the prose and was wrong about the quotations.** The remaining failures are prose sentences MiniCheck does not consider supported by their cited passage, which is ungrounded answer generation, not a fault in the check.

Verification: `ruff check` and `ruff format --check` pass; 9 tests in `tests/test_safeguard_sentences.py`.
