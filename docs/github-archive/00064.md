### Affected App, Adopter, Users, or Systems

Every visitor who asks a question on the home page (/) and listens to the memo and the map tour. /map is affected by the ordering part.

### Observed Consequence and Workaround

Observed (live, ?v=ux1, example question "Automobile exception: probable cause standard in Colorado"):
- The memo plays to the end, then the first tour clip is **[2] People v. Cox**. Source [1] People v. Chamberlain is never narrated. [3] and [7] are skipped too, although the memo cites [1], [3], [4] and [7].
- While the memo is still playing, the map flies to the first stop and types its caption silently, then moves on to the next stop. When the memo ends the tour jumps back.

Root cause (two parts, both in the static tour code):
1. **Second `Docs3D.show()` from the activity feed.** `render()` in `static/index.html` calls `Docs3D.show(result)` and starts the memo, but never sets `window.__early`. The activity feed then replays the `ranked` event, sees `window.__early !== run_id`, and calls `Docs3D.show(e.citations, e.edges, [])` again about 1.5 s later. That call (a) swaps the full citations for the ranked-event copies, whose passages are cut at 600 characters and which have no documents, and (b) re-arms `show()`'s own autostart timer (`tour=setTimeout(()=>api.tour(), 1000+…)` in `static/map3d.js`). Nothing cancels that timer, so a silent caption-only tour starts while the memo holds the voice.
2. **`Docs3D.order()` drops sources.** The tour keeps only sources whose passage yields a complete "holding" sentence (or that another source cites) and puts those first. The sentence splitter breaks on "v." and "e.g.", and with the 600-character passages [1], [3] and [7] have no holding, so they disappear and the stop list becomes [2], [4], [6], [5]. Even with full passages the order is [1], [2], [3], [4], [6], [7], [5], not [n] order.

When the memo ends, `Docs3D.tour(0)` narrates `order()[0]`, which is [2]. So stop 1 is skipped. The audio queue, the memo wait from #19/#21 and the Fish/Kokoro/browser chain behave correctly; the narrated clip is simply the wrong first stop.

Workaround: press the ◀ (previous stop) control or click pin [1] after the tour starts.
