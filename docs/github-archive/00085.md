### Observed Consequence and Workaround

Observed: on the answer map card the 'Agents retrieving across U.S. and Colorado courts…' status overlaps 'Open full map' and stays after the answer is cached or done; the tour controls (prev, sound, Tour, next) sit on top of the step dots 1-8 and dots 7-8 are cut off; the legend and title overlay cover much of the map; the legend says '0 counties linked' while pins show; the 'Follow up:' chips take a lot of space.
